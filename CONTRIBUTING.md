# Contributing

This project exists to improve technical claim discipline in autonomy and
denied-environment engineering. Contributions should make claims easier to
bound, reproduce, challenge, or honestly downgrade.

## Good contributions

- clearer definitions of maturity and evidence classes;
- public-safe test and evidence templates;
- domain-specific examples with stated assumptions;
- references to standards or primary technical literature;
- corrections that improve precision without increasing hostility;
- tooling that checks whether a claim includes conditions and limits.

## Out of scope

- naming and shaming individual founders or companies;
- doxxing, private records, or NDA circumvention;
- treating lack of public evidence as proof of fraud;
- turning the rubric into a certification, procurement recommendation, or
  technical product;
- adding universal claims that the rubric itself would reject.

## Review standard

Every change should answer:

1. What claim or decision does this help a reader evaluate?
2. What assumptions and limits does it make explicit?
3. Does it apply equally to the maintainers' own work?
4. Can it be used without revealing protected implementation details?

Please keep the tone factual. The project critiques claim/evidence mismatch,
not people.
